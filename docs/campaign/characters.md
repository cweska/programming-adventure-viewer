# Character notes

This file lists what came from each character sheet and what was filled in or changed, so the teacher can review it. Student names are never recorded here or anywhere else in the repo.

The setting rule for every character: they were all carried into this fantasy world from somewhere else. Modern guns belong to their old lives and did not cross over. Firearms in this world are steampunk machines powered by runes and magic, and they can be introduced later. Combat in this pass is melee only.

## Gearshift (Alien Robot, Warrior / Tank)

**From the sheet**
- Name, species, class/role, and Good alignment.
- About 20 feet tall. Turns into a backpack weapon system and a jeep.
- Ability scores: STR 17, CON 17, ATH 17, DEX 13, PERS 13, PER 14, INT 14, WIS 15, MAG 10.
- Innate: short-range teleport, optical illusions and camouflage, realistic holograms.
- Martial: mechanic and technician, Muay Thai, medical work, improvising with little.
- Magical: self-healing over time, fire shots, transformation.
- Weakness: everything runs on a rare ore found almost only on his home world. If it runs out, he dies.
- Gear: sword, metal-plate skin, and a forcefield that uses half his energy.
- Backstory: hauling cargo in an intergalactic war, boarded by the enemy, crashed in an escape pod, sent a signal for survivors.

**Filled in or changed**
- The sheet's overview left the name blank, so the name comes from its JSON block. The JSON's broken CON entry is read as 17.
- The home nation is renamed to the original **Corvex Prime**, because the sheet's nation name comes from an existing franchise. The faction keeps the sheet's name, the Freedom Keepers.
- The rare ore is named **starlode ore**: fuel, ammunition, and medicine in one. "Dies" is softened to "shuts down for good".
- His three-barrel musket and compact rifle are old-world lore only. He keeps all of his non-gun tech, ready for future modules.
- Appearance is based on the sheet's drawing: boxy steel body, star shoulder pads, twin shoulder stacks, a visor face, and wheel-and-tread legs.
- Game stats: longsword d8, metal hide +3 armor.

**Story flag:** At 20 feet tall, Gearshift cannot fit through the door of Mar'Tu's small cabin. His backpack form might explain how he gets inside. That is your call.

## Master Oogrish (Turtle, General / Leader)

**From the sheet**
- Name, species, class, and Good alignment.
- Ability scores from the sheet's JSON block: STR 13, CON 14, ATH 13, DEX 14, PERS 14, PER 10, INT 17, WIS 17, MAG 9.
- Innate: none. Magical: none. Martial: guns.
- Relic: the Staff of Wisdom. Faction: the Peace Temple.
- Backstory: fought beside a friend who grew greedy for power, then had to defeat him and became a wise weapons master.

**Filled in or changed**
- The JSON's "Human" race and "Character Name" were template leftovers. The sheet's overview (Turtle) is used instead.
- **General** is a new class: a leader with 10 base HP. Command abilities are left for a later module.
- The rival's name came from an existing film, so it is changed to the original **Varkos**.
- The sheet's last line about what he taught is changed to "taught others to protect the weak".
- The two weapons from a video game become old-world lore only (a rapid-fire combat shotgun and a heavy six-shot revolver). "Firearms master" stays as a martial skill for when steampunk guns arrive.
- Garments, which were left blank: a weathered general's sash and a woven reed cloak over his shell.
- Weakness, which was left blank: slow to turn and climb in his shell, and too trusting of old friends.
- Appearance: mossy green shell, long white mustache, calm amber eyes.
- The golden clockwork medallion from the Module 1 outline is added as a relic.
- Game stats: Staff of Wisdom d6, shell and cloak +2 armor.
